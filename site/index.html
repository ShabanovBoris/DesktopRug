A very familiar cleanup routine.

Clean enough.
Until you peek.

Drag the rug over your icons, pull up a corner, and watch the fabric settle. It’s a visual cleanup. Your files stay exactly where they are.

01 / SWEEP

Put the mess out of sight.

Drag the middle to cover desktop icons. The rug remembers where you left it.

02 / PEEK

Take a peek underneath.

Drag a corner to lift and fold the cloth. Option-drag to grab any point. The mess is still there.

03 / REVEAL

Find your files again.

Right-click to reveal and use the original icons. Lock the rug when you want clicks to pass through. No files are moved or deleted.

macOS blocked the first launch?

The current download is ad-hoc signed and isn’t notarized. After trying to open it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway if you trust the download. Apple’s instructions ↗. You can also build from source.

What does Finder access do?

Optional Finder integration reads icon positions to make the fabric drape over them. It asks for Automation access. The app never moves, edits, or deletes your files; it needs no Accessibility or Screen Recording permission. Manual collision mounds work without Finder access.

Will this work on my setup?

The released binary requires macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon. One rug appears on one selected display. Automatic Finder collisions work on the main display; Stacks and arrangement modes can limit icon positions. Spaces, Stage Manager, and fullscreen transitions haven’t been fully verified.
